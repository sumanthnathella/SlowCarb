## Read the labels in this guide

- **Fits the framework:** consistent with the protein, pulses, and vegetables approach.
- **Modify:** the dish or product needs ingredient changes.
- **Adaptation:** a practical interpretation, not something Ferriss explicitly approved.
- **Outside the strict plan:** leave out on the six diet days. This does **not** mean the food is unhealthy.

!!! note "A corrected guide, not a promise"
    Ferriss's rules are not the same as established nutrition evidence. This guide does not recommend binge eating, rapid-loss promises, or supplement stacks. Gluten and lactose considerations apply every day. [Read the framework](principles.md) and [sources](sources.md) before treating a classification as a health claim.
