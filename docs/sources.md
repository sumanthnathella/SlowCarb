# Evidence & sources

**Separate the author's rules, our adaptations, and nutrition evidence.** A food can be outside the Ferriss plan while remaining a nutritious choice in a different eating pattern.

## Primary book source

Timothy Ferriss, *The 4-Hour Body* (2010):

- **“The Slow-Carb Diet I”** — the five-rule framework, example food groups, and meal repetition.
- **“The Slow-Carb Diet II”** — vegetarian FAQ, dairy exceptions, protein guidance, vegetable flexibility, and common questions.
- **“The Meatless Machine” chapters** — broader vegetarian discussion. Their meal plans are not automatically slow-carb fat-loss menus.

These pages contain original summaries, not copied chapters. The source EPUB is not included in this repository or website. Chapter titles are used because page numbers vary by edition.

Ferriss does not classify every Indian vegetable, millet, or packaged product. Entries such as arvi, suran, rice-free pesarattu, tofu-based meals, and modified avial apply his framework or adapt it; they are not presented as quotations or explicit endorsements.

## Corrections carried into this guide

| Earlier oversimplification | What to rely on instead |
| --- | --- |
| The diet reliably produces 20 pounds of fat loss in a month | This is not a routine expectation or promise. Early scale changes can include water. |
| A weekly binge is required to reset metabolism | A binge is not necessary for weight loss; our flexible day is an intentional departure from the book. |
| Dairy's insulin response proves dairy prevents fat loss | Insulin response alone does not establish that conclusion. Dairy-free defaults here address tolerance and simplify meal planning. |
| Tofu in a nutrient table makes all soy explicitly approved | The book discourages refined soy in its vegetarian FAQ. Tofu is labeled a practical adaptation here. |
| Legumes must be included at every meal | The book allows omission in some meals. Overall protein, energy, and adequacy still matter. |
| Whole fruit and millets are unhealthy | Their exclusion is a feature of this particular diet, not a general health verdict. |
| Everyone needs potassium, calcium, and high-dose magnesium pills | Supplement decisions should reflect dietary intake, medical conditions, and professional advice. |
| Gluten or lactose restrictions can be ignored on an off day | Dietary safety and tolerance apply every day. Gluten avoidance in celiac disease is lifelong. |

## Independent nutrition and health references

### Weight management

[CDC: Steps for Losing Weight](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html)

Supports gradual, sustainable changes and describes roughly 1–2 pounds per week as a general pace more likely to be maintained than faster loss. It is not an individualized goal for every person. Energy balance remains relevant; no approved-food list guarantees a calorie deficit.

### Soy and vegetarian protein

[Harvard Nutrition Source: Straight Talk About Soy](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/soy/)

Reviews soy nutrition and evidence. Ordinary soy foods are useful protein sources for many people; blanket claims that soy is harmful are not warranted. Soy allergy is a separate consideration.

[Harvard Health: Tofu—Making a Place for This Nutritious Plant-Based Food in Your Diet](https://www.health.harvard.edu/diet-and-nutrition/tofu-making-a-place-for-this-nutritious-plant-based-food-in-your-diet)

Discusses protein, calcium, and variation between tofu products. This guide's protein figures are approximate; actual labels and cooked ingredient quantities are more useful than treating all tofu or all dal as equivalent.

### Gluten and cross-contact

[NIDDK: Eating, Diet, & Nutrition for Celiac Disease](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/celiac-disease/eating-diet-nutrition)

Explains lifelong gluten avoidance for celiac disease, labeling, cross-contact, and nutritional planning. Being symptom-free is not proof that gluten exposure is safe for someone with celiac disease.

[American Gastroenterological Association: Preventing Gluten Cross-Contact](https://patient.gastro.org/preventing-gluten-cross-contact/)

Practical handling guidance, including checking legumes for stray gluten-containing grains. Sorting and rinsing are useful precautions, not a guarantee that an already contaminated product is safe.

### Cooking beans and storing leftovers

[FDA: Bad Bug Book, second edition (PDF)](https://www.fda.gov/files/food/published/Bad-Bug-Book-2nd-Edition-(PDF).pdf)

The phytohaemagglutinin section explains kidney-bean lectin poisoning and preparation precautions, including soaking and vigorous boiling. A slow cooker alone is not a safe method for raw dried kidney beans.

[USDA FSIS: Leftovers and Food Safety](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)

Supports prompt refrigeration, shallow containers, the 3–4 day refrigerated-leftover window, and reheating to 74°C/165°F. Freeze extra portions rather than keeping a full week's cooked food in the refrigerator.

## Product information

The [grocery guide](groceries/index.md) links individual manufacturer ingredient lists and reports the limits of store access. Research date: **20 September 2026**.

- A manufacturer's catalog is evidence that a product is listed, not evidence of local stock.
- An online ingredient list is not a gluten-free certification.
- Package formulations, labels, and availability may change.
- Fresh or frozen food classifications in this guide are separate from clinical dietary safety.

## Scope and privacy

This is a general vegetarian, gluten-aware, dairy-free-by-default planning reference. It does not diagnose gluten intolerance, celiac disease, lactose intolerance, allergies, or nutritional deficiencies. Seek individualized advice when medical conditions, medicines, pregnancy, or disordered eating make restrictions more complex.

Keep personal medical details, food-reaction logs, weight records, addresses, and credentials out of a public repository. A private source repository does not by itself guarantee that a GitHub Pages website is private.
