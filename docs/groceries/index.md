# Fresh & frozen shopping

**Shop by the ingredient, then check the actual package.** Frozen vegetables can be an excellent convenience choice. Freezing does not turn a tuber into a non-starchy vegetable or make a prepared dish gluten-free.

Catalog research checked **20 September 2026**. Ingredient lists can change. These are diet-framework interpretations, not product certifications or medical safety guarantees.

### Frozen legumes, not just vegetable sides

| Package name | What it is | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green Channa | Green chickpeas | A legume curry or sundal-style dish |
| Tuvar Lilva | Green pigeon peas | Curry or stew |
| Surti Papdi Lilva | Shelled flat-bean seeds | Legume curry |

**Papdi and papdi lilva are different shopping terms:** pods versus shelled beans. Both can fit, but their nutritional roles and cooking differ. Fresh green legumes also differ from mature dried pulses; don't assign every serving the same protein count.

### Outside the strict-day shopping list

Deep also lists **arvi/taro, suran/elephant-foot yam, ratalu, tapioca/cassava, diced potatoes, cooked dum aloo, and sweetcorn**. Leave these out of this strict-day adaptation. A grocery category called “vegetables” is not a diet classification.

Lotus root and singoda/water chestnuts also appear in the catalog. These are starchier choices, not automatic substitutes for non-starchy gourds. They are not part of this starter plan; Ferriss does not supply a clear individual classification for them.

## Mixed bags: the ingredient list changes the answer

### Deep Sambhar Mix, 12 oz — fits the vegetable framework

The checked list contains **tomatoes, drumsticks, eggplant/brinjal, onions, okra, bottle gourd, green chillies, and curry leaves**.

Use it to simplify prep. **Add dal separately:** this is a vegetable mix, not a protein-complete sambar or ready meal.

[Manufacturer ingredients](https://deepfoods.com/products/frozen-produce/frozen-vegetables/sambhar-mix-12oz-diq21)

### Deep Aviyal Mix, 14 oz — not suitable as-is for strict days

The checked list contains **yams**, drumsticks, carrots, cucumbers, long beans, snake gourd, green mangoes, green chillies, garlic, and fried curry leaves.

The yam is enough to keep this mix out of our strict-day plan. Instead combine individual suitable vegetables for [modified avial](../meals/recipes.md). This frozen vegetable mix is not the same as a finished yogurt-containing avial; check both the bag and what you add when cooking.

[Manufacturer ingredients](https://deepfoods.com/products/frozen-produce/frozen-vegetables/aviyal-mix-14oz-diq79)

### Deep Mixed Vegetables, 3.85 lb — choose another mix for this trial

The checked ingredients are **carrots, sweetcorn, green peas, and French beans**. We leave out this corn-containing blend for the strict trial. That does not mean a little corn is dangerous or that every brand's mixed vegetables has the same contents.

[Manufacturer ingredients](https://deepfoods.com/products/frozen-produce/frozen-vegetables/mixed-vegetables-385lb-diq64)

### Deep Surti Undhiu Mix, 12 oz — gluten warning, plus strict-plan mismatch

The list contains flat beans, potatoes, sweet potatoes, eggplant, violet yams, and fenugreek dumplings. The dumplings include **wheat flour**, alongside chickpea flour and other ingredients.

**Avoid this product for gluten intolerance based on that ingredient list.** It also contains multiple strict-plan starch exclusions. “Vegan” does not mean gluten-free. Do not try to make a mixed product gluten-free by picking out the dumplings.

[Manufacturer ingredients](https://deepfoods.com/products/frozen-produce/frozen-vegetables/surti-undhiu-mix-12oz-diq31)

## Fresh produce shopping vocabulary

For fresh produce, search the [complete Indian vegetable reference](../foods/vegetables.md). Useful terms beyond the verified frozen catalog include:

- **Ridge gourd / turai / peerkangai / beerakaya.**
- **Ash gourd / white pumpkin / poosanikai / boodida gummadikaya.**
- **Chayote / chow-chow / Bangalore brinjal.**
- **Long beans / yardlong beans / karamani pods.**
- **Dosakaya / cooking cucumber / Mangalore cucumber.**
- **Gongura, Malabar spinach / basale / bachali, and moringa leaves.**
- **Banana stem / vazhaithandu and banana flower / vazhaipoo.**
- **Radish / mullangi, brinjal / vankaya, and knol-khol / noolkol.**

These are shopping search suggestions, **not verified stock at any chain**. Regional names can vary; match the vegetable or ingredient description as well as its name.

## A useful starter freezer basket

Pick **four to six bags**, not the entire catalog:

1. Palak or thotakura for dal.
2. Bhindi or tindora for a dry vegetable side.
3. Guvar, fansi, or chauri for usili/poriyal.
4. Dudhi or snake gourd for kootu.
5. Sambhar Mix for convenience.
6. Green channa or tuvar lilva if you want a frozen legume option.

Add your preferred dry/canned pulses and a separate protein such as tofu or optional eggs. A freezer full of vegetables alone is not a complete vegetarian meal plan.

## Four checks before putting a bag in the cart

1. **Ingredient:** plain vegetable, legume, starch, or prepared dish?
2. **Recipe:** added potato, flour, rice, sugar, cream, yogurt, or breading?
3. **Intolerance:** gluten-free labeling and allergen/cross-contact information appropriate to your needs? Lactose or milk ingredients?
4. **Use:** a protein serving, pulse serving, vegetable side, or seasoning?

Follow package cooking and storage directions; frozen produce is not necessarily ready to eat. Keep cold food cold and cook it appropriately.
