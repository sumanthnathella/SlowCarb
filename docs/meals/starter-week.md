# A repeatable starter week

Start with six repeating diet days and an **optional flexible day**. This is a practical vegetarian adaptation, not the book’s exact unrestricted-day protocol. Every default meal remains egg-free, gluten-free, and dairy-free; flexibility never requires eating an intolerance trigger or deliberately bingeing.

## Seven-day menu

Use the [recipe portions](recipes.md), not tiny tasting servings. Sauté chopped cabbage or cauliflower in oil with cumin and salt for the vegetable base.

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mung-only pesarattu + tofu bhurji; optional avocado | Ridge gourd pappu + 150 g tofu + cabbage | Bean usili + 200 g tofu + cauliflower |
| 2 | Repeat breakfast | Repeat lunch | Repeat dinner |
| 3 | Repeat breakfast | Repeat lunch | Repeat dinner |
| 4 | Fresh-batch pesarattu + tofu bhurji | Fresh-batch pappu + tofu + cabbage | Fresh-batch usili + tofu + cauliflower |
| 5 | Repeat day 4 | Repeat day 4 | Repeat day 4; optional adapted avial side |
| 6 | Repeat day 4 | Repeat day 4 | Repeat day 4 |
| 7 | Same breakfast, or another tolerated gluten-free, dairy-free breakfast | Leftover portions frozen earlier, or a checked restaurant curry bowl | Usual meal or a flexible gluten-free, dairy-free choice |

Day 7 can simply repeat a diet day. A grain-based gluten-free meal may be a flexible choice, but is not relabeled slow-carb. Resume your normal pattern without compensatory fasting or exercise.

## One-person shopping: first three days

This makes **1½ batches of pesarattu and bhurji**, one pappu batch, and one usili batch. Buy more only for larger appetites or additional sides.

| Item | Approximate amount |
| --- | --- |
| Firm tofu | 1.5 kg total: 450 g breakfast, 450 g lunch, 600 g dinner; check drained weights |
| Dry pulses | Whole mung 150 g; split yellow moong 200 g; chana dal 150 g |
| Main vegetables | Ridge gourd 600 g; green beans 600 g; cabbage 600 g; cauliflower 600 g, usable weights |
| Bhurji and pappu vegetables | Onion 250 g; tomato 500 g; spinach 150 g |
| Optional extras | One small avocado; one lemon |
| Pantry check | Oil, salt, cumin, mustard seeds, turmeric, ginger, curry leaves, chili; gluten-free spices |

Avial ingredients are not included; shop separately if adding that side later.

## Day one: a usable sequence

1. **The night before:** refrigerate whole mung and chana dal in separate bowls of soaking water. Check tofu and spice labels.
2. **Breakfast:** blend the mung and cook the pancakes. Make bhurji; eat one-third of your three-day breakfast batch. Refrigerate the remaining cooked portions promptly.
3. **Lunch:** cook the pappu batch and cabbage. Warm 150 g tofu for today’s serving; portion the remaining pappu for days 2 and 3.
4. **Dinner:** steam the usili pulse mixture, cook beans, and combine. Prepare cauliflower and warm 200 g tofu. Portion leftovers into shallow containers.
5. **Before day 4:** shop and cook again, or use portions frozen promptly after an earlier batch. Follow the [storage guidance](recipes.md) rather than refrigerating seven days of food.

!!! tip "Enough food, gradual changes"
    These are starting portions, not calorie limits. If pulses are new to you, begin with smaller servings and increase gradually; use more tolerated tofu rather than forcing several cups of legumes per meal. Eat more when hungry, including extra meal portions or a tolerated snack. Hunger is not failure. Protein depends on your tofu label and actual portions, not the menu name.

Weight, activity, and medical history are unknown, so this plan does not prescribe daily calories or macros. For pregnancy, kidney disease, glucose-lowering medication, or persistent digestive symptoms, seek individualized advice.
