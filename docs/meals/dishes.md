# South Indian dishes: keep, adapt, or replace

Replace rice and wheat **without removing most of the meal**. This guide defaults to egg-free, gluten-free, and dairy-free meals. Tofu is a practical vegetarian adaptation, not a blanket Ferriss-approved category. “Adapt” means these recipes, not every traditional version.

See [principles](../principles.md), [vegetables](../foods/vegetables.md), and [sources](../sources.md).

!!! note "Protein before substitutions"
    Ferriss describes roughly 20 g protein per meal and 30 g at breakfast: book rules, not individualized requirements. Meals around 25–30 g are feasible with substantial cooked pulse portions plus tofu. Check tofu labels. Optional eggs can replace some tofu, but one egg is not equivalent to a substantial tofu portion.

## Searchable dish reference

| Dish / alternate name | Practical fit | What to check or change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sambar | Keep, pulse-forward | Use substantial toor dal, not mostly broth. Add drumstick, brinjal, radish, or gourds; omit potato and jaggery. Check spice blends. |
| Rasam / saaru | Side, not protein anchor | Usually little dal. Pair with pulses, tofu, and vegetables. A little culinary tamarind is an adaptation here. |
| Pappu / dal | Keep | Moong or toor dal with greens, ridge gourd, or tomato. Use oil instead of ghee; add vegetables and tofu instead of rice. |
| Kootu | Adapt | Pulses, non-starchy vegetables, modest coconut; skip rice flour, potato, yam, and dairy. Add protein if mostly vegetables. |
| Poriyal / thoran / palya | Vegetable side | Choose cabbage, beans, greens, or cauliflower; garnish with coconut. Skip potato, raw banana, and yam versions on diet days. |
| Paruppu usili | Keep | Steamed pulse crumbles with beans. Avoid rice flour and deep-frying; add tofu for a fuller meal. |
| Sundal | Keep | Well-cooked chickpeas, mung, or black-eyed peas. Modest coconut, no added sugar. A small snack cup is not a complete meal. |
| Avial / aviyal | Adapt; side | Ash gourd, drumstick, long beans, snake gourd, modest carrot and coconut, little tamarind. No yam, taro, raw banana, potato, or yogurt. Add separate protein. |
| Pesarattu | Rice-free adaptation | Mung only: some recipes add rice or an upma filling. Pair with tofu bhurji instead. |
| Adai / chilla | All-pulse adaptation | Rice-free all-pulse adai or besan chilla. Check mixes for rice, wheat, or semolina; cook thoroughly. |
| Idli / dosa / uttapam | Replace on diet days | Standard batters contain rice. Millet/ragi versions may be gluten-free but remain grain-based. Fermentation does not remove grain. |
| Pongal / upma / poha / mixed rice | Replace on diet days | Rice, semolina, flattened rice, millet, and ragi are not pulses. Try dal over cabbage or cauliflower. |
| Mor kuzhambu | Skip yogurt version | Lactose-free yogurt is still dairy. Choose a separate dairy-free curry. |
| Vegetable korma / kurma | Adapt | Tofu, non-starchy vegetables, moderate coconut; no potato, milk, cream, or yogurt. Check restaurant ingredients. |
| Vada / bajji / pakora / breaded cutlets | Not regular meal anchors | Avoid routine deep-fried meals. Breading may contain wheat; fryers may be shared. Choose steamed usili or pan-cooked pulse pancakes. |

## Familiar meals, rebuilt

- **Avocado toast →** rice-free pesarattu, a little avocado, and tofu bhurji. Avocado is a garnish, not the protein.
- **Rice, dal, vegetables →** keep dal and vegetables, add tofu or optional eggs, and replace rice with sautéed cabbage or cauliflower. Do not make the meal tiny.
- **Gluten-free pasta or pizza →** curry bowls with pappu or sambar, tofu, and vegetables. Gluten-free grain products are not automatically slow-carb.

!!! warning "Ingredient checks"
    Compounded asafoetida/hing often contains wheat: choose explicitly gluten-free hing or omit it. Check blends, batters, sauces, and shared surfaces. Request no ghee, butter, cream, milk, paneer, or yogurt. Little lemon or tamarind used as seasoning is a practical exception, not a blanket author-approved fruit allowance.
